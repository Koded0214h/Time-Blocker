# Time Blocker

### Time is an asset. Keep it.

TimeOS is an AI-powered personal operating system that automatically plans, adapts, and optimizes your time around your goals, commitments, habits, and changing life events.

Unlike traditional calendars and planners, TimeOS eliminates the friction of manual time blocking. Instead of spending time creating schedules, users simply connect their calendars, import their routines, define their priorities, and let TimeOS build an optimized day automatically.

---

## The Problem

Managing time is harder than ever.

Most productivity tools require users to:

* Manually create calendar events
* Drag and resize blocks
* Rebuild schedules when plans change
* Balance work, school, fitness, faith, and personal goals

The result is that many people spend more time planning than actually doing.

TimeOS solves this problem by acting as a personal AI scheduler that continuously manages time on behalf of the user.

---

## The Solution

TimeOS learns a user's lifestyle, priorities, commitments, and energy patterns to automatically generate realistic daily schedules.

The system:

* Imports existing calendar events
* Detects scheduling conflicts
* Creates optimized time blocks
* Adapts when plans change
* Reschedules unfinished tasks
* Learns from user behavior over time

The goal is simple:

> Stop managing your schedule. Start living it.

---

## Core Features

### Smart Onboarding

Instead of asking users to manually create schedules, TimeOS gathers context through:

* Calendar integrations
* Academic timetable uploads
* Habit preferences
* Personal goals
* Work and school commitments

This creates a personal blueprint used to generate schedules automatically.

---

### AI Schedule Generation

TimeOS automatically creates daily schedules based on:

* Existing calendar events
* Recurring habits
* Deadlines
* User goals
* Available time
* Energy levels

Users wake up to a schedule that is already planned.

---

### Calendar Synchronization

Integrates with:

* Google Calendar
* Apple Calendar
* Outlook Calendar

Features include:

* Conflict detection
* Automatic event synchronization
* Intelligent schedule adjustments

---

### Dynamic Replanning

Life changes.

TimeOS adapts.

If a user misses a block or encounters a new commitment, TimeOS automatically:

* Reschedules tasks
* Rebalances priorities
* Preserves important commitments
* Optimizes the remaining day

---

### AI Chat Assistant

Users can talk to TimeOS naturally.

Examples:

* "Exams start in 2 weeks."
* "I have a basketball tournament next month."
* "Help me prepare for my React interview."
* "I want to launch my startup before August."

TimeOS adjusts schedules and priorities accordingly.

---

### Daily Review System

At the end of each day, users can quickly review completed blocks.

Tasks can be:

* Completed
* Partially completed
* Missed

Missed tasks are intelligently rescheduled into future schedules.

---

### Energy Tracking

TimeOS learns when users perform best.

Examples:

* Deep work hours
* Study hours
* Workout hours
* Low-energy periods

Over time, schedules become increasingly personalized and effective.
